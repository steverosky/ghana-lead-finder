# Ghana Outreach Templates

Short, WhatsApp-first openers for the qualified leads. WhatsApp is the dominant business channel in Ghana — keep messages brief, polite, and concrete. Only use these when the user asks for outreach help.

## WhatsApp — social-only business (no website)

> Hello 👋 I came across {business} on {Facebook/Instagram} — love what you're doing in {area}. I build simple, mobile-friendly websites for businesses like yours so customers can find you on Google and order/book directly (with WhatsApp + MoMo). Would a quick look at a sample be useful?

## WhatsApp — weak / free-builder site

> Hi {business}, I saw your page online. The site loads slowly on phone and customers can't easily contact or book you. I help local businesses fix this for a one-time setup. Can I send you a 60-second before/after example?

## WhatsApp — active seller with no real store

> Hello! Your {product/service} posts are great and clearly selling. Right now buyers have to DM and wait — a simple site with a catalog + WhatsApp checkout + MoMo would let you sell while you sleep. Want me to mock one up for {business}?

## Phone-call opener (if user prefers calling)

> Good morning, please am I speaking with the owner of {business}? My name is {name}, I help businesses in {area} get a proper website so customers find them on Google instead of only Facebook. Is now a good time for two minutes?

## Notes

- Lead with a benefit a Ghanaian SME cares about: **be found on Google**, **sell on WhatsApp**, **accept MoMo**, **look credible**.
- Avoid jargon ("CMS", "SEO funnel"). Say "found on Google", "works on phone", "customers can order".
- Mention a one-time/affordable setup; many SMEs fear ongoing foreign-currency subscriptions.
- Best contact times: weekday mornings (8–11am) or after 5pm; avoid Sundays for church-going owners.
